Codebook

Four semantic fields for deciding what kind of meaning an English nominal foregrounds when it becomes a Chinese verb in the aligned subtitle.

Scope Categories Communication Cognition Institutions Everyday Change log

Scope and unit

The coding unit is one NV Atlas sentence record with an English nominal and a Chinese verbal rendition. I classify the meaning foregrounded by the English nominal in its sentence. The Chinese verb helps confirm that the aligned phrase is a process rather than a literal nominal equivalent, but its word shape does not determine the category.

The codebook combines Halliday and Matthiessen's distinction among material, mental, verbal, and relational process types with Vinay and Darbelnet's concept of transposition. “Institutions and rules” is an applied category for official or procedural contexts in this subtitle sample; it does not claim to be a separate transitivity process.

Priority rule. If a noun could fit two categories, first decide whether the sentence explicitly frames the event as an official or institutional procedure. If not, choose communication for saying/writing/ representing, cognition for sensing/feeling/knowing, and everyday for all remaining actions and states.

Change log

  1. Draft 1 (24-entry pre-test). I began with action, state, and communication. The pre-test showed that legal procedure and permission repeatedly crossed those boundaries, so a fourth applied category was needed.
  2. Draft 2 (theory alignment). I aligned the first three fields with material, mental, and verbal meaning, while retaining an institutional field for this corpus's visible legal and administrative content. I clarified that the “everyday” field is residual.
  3. Draft 3 (context rules). During the 50-entry check, terms such as administration, government, sanction, and revocation were moved to institutions. Consideration was moved to communication when used in the formula “for your consideration”, and insistence was treated as a communicative claim.
  4. Data cleaning. One malformed apostrophe sequence was normalised to a typographic apostrophe. The 50 checked source IDs and sentence pairs otherwise matched the public NV Atlas records.

範圍與分析單位

分析單位是一條 NV Atlas 句對紀錄，包含一個英語名詞及其中文動詞譯法。 分類對象是該名詞在句中所突顯的意義。中文動詞有助確認對齊詞語 表達過程而非名詞直譯，但中文詞形本身不決定類別。

碼本結合 Halliday 與 Matthiessen 對物質、心理、言語及關係過程的區分， 以及 Vinay 與 Darbelnet 的「詞性轉換」概念。「制度與規則」是因應本字幕 樣本中明顯的法律及行政語境而設的應用類別，並不宣稱是另一種及物性過程。

優先規則。當名詞可以落入兩類時，先判斷句子是否明確把 事件框定為官方或制度程序；若不是，說、寫、表達歸入溝通，感知、情感、 認知歸入心理，其餘行動與狀態歸入日常。

認知與情感 名詞突顯感知、思想、信念、願望或情緒，即 Halliday 所稱的 心理過程。 當英語名詞描述內在經驗，或注意力和情緒狀態的改變時選用。 + disappointment → 失望
− permission → 允许（被允許的行動或權利） 		attention → 注意。要求別人留意的命令仍涉及感知與注意力， 因此即使具社交指令功能，仍歸入此類。

修改紀錄

  1. 第一稿（24 條預試）。初稿只有行動、狀態及溝通。 預試發現法律程序與許可經常跨越界線，因此增加第四個應用類別。
  2. 第二稿（理論對齊）。把前三類對應物質、心理及言語 意義，同時保留制度類以處理語料中明顯的法律及行政內容，並清楚說明 「日常」是剩餘類別。
  3. 第三稿（語境規則）。核對 50 條時， administration、government、sanction、 revocation 等詞改歸制度類；consideration 在 “for your consideration” 公式中改歸溝通類；insistence 被視為主張，歸入溝通類。
  4. 資料清理。把一組錯誤編碼的撇號正規化為正確標點； 其餘 50 條已核對的來源編號及句對均與公開 NV Atlas 紀錄相符。
