When English nouns return to action in Chinese

A 500-pair study of nominal-to-verbal shifts in subtitles, built from the Department of Translation's NV Atlas and its OpenSubtitles 2024 source.

Origin Background Gap Questions Method Analysis Conclusion Reflection References

Origin

I chose this topic because the difference between an English nominal and a Chinese verb is small enough to inspect line by line, but visible enough to build a larger argument around. Subtitle dialogue is also a useful reminder that translation is not a word-for-word copying task: translators compress, reframe, and redistribute information to make a line sound natural in the target language.

The project began from the course brief and the public NV Atlas interface. Rather than treating the atlas as a black box, I wanted a smaller, reproducible slice whose search, categories, and unresolved boundaries could all be examined on one site.

Background

English academic and formal prose often condenses a process into a noun, as in the construction of the tower. Halliday and Matthiessen describe this as grammatical metaphor: a process is represented as an entity. Chinese can retain that nominal, but it may also return the process to a verb, producing a more dynamic clause.

Vinay and Darbelnet's concept of transposition provides a practical description of the local word-class change. The corpus narrows this wider idea to one recurring direction: an English nominal is associated with a Chinese verbal rendition in the aligned subtitle line. The project then asks which kinds of meaning are most often pulled back into verbs.

Gap

NV Atlas already exposes a large collection of nominal-verb links and several powerful visualizations. Its scale is a strength, but it also makes it difficult for a visitor to see one bounded sample, the sampling decisions behind it, and the places where a semantic codebook becomes uncertain.

This project fills that smaller pedagogical gap. It publishes a fixed random sample, one reproducible selection method, four transparent categories, and a linked interface where a chart observation can be checked immediately against sentence pairs. It does not claim to replace NV Atlas; it turns part of the source into a portable case study.

Questions

  1. Which semantic fields dominate a fixed 500-pair sample of English nominal-to-Chinese verbal shifts?
  2. How consistent are the term-to-verb links across repeated English nominals such as attention, protection, and permission?
  3. Do source and target sentence lengths vary by semantic category?
  4. Which codebook boundaries remain difficult, and what do those boundaries reveal about the data?

Method

Corpus and sample

On 5 November 2026, I extracted the records exposed by the public NV Atlas search page. NV Atlas identifies OpenSubtitles v2024 as its source and documents POS tagging, Jieba segmentation, Gemma 3 assistance, and human inspection in its pipeline. The public search payload contained 20,965 sentence records at the time of collection.

The filters were fixed before sampling:

  • both English and Chinese sides must be non-empty;
  • the English side must contain at least three consecutive letters;
  • the Chinese side must contain Chinese characters;
  • English must be 10–250 characters and Chinese at most 300 characters;
  • the record must contain at least one nominal-verb annotation;
  • exact English-Chinese sentence-pair duplicates are removed.

The filters rejected 167 length outliers and left 20,798 candidates. Python's random.Random(2026).sample() then drew 500 candidates without replacement. The first nominal-verb annotation returned for each sentence became the row's key_term and target_key_term. Every row keeps the NV Atlas sentence ID, source label, collection date, and sample seed.

Classification and check

I applied the four-category codebook on the codebook page. Category decisions focus on what the English nominal profiles in its sentence, not on the form of the Chinese word alone. A second fixed-seed selection (seed 2027) marked 50 entries for source matching and closer category review. The IDs and text matched the public source; five borderline category labels and one encoding artefact were corrected during that review.

Reliability limitation: the labelling workflow was AI-assisted and rule-based. It is reproducible, but it is not equivalent to independent human double annotation. Before the oral presentation, I must re-read the 50 checked entries and be ready to defend the five boundary decisions.

Website

The site is static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and JSON. Search and three filters share one state object. Changes update the network, scatter plot, bilingual KWIC, heat map, and data grid. Clicking a term enters it into search, and the query string preserves the keyword, filters, selected term, network controls, sorting, and page number.

Analysis

The sample is dominated by everyday actions and states, but the large total partly reflects the broadness of that residual category. The category counts should be read together with the codebook boundaries and the table, not as four equal-scale properties.

Nominal–verb network

The Nominal–verb network makes repetition visible. attention → 注意 is the strongest repeated edge (27 occurrences), followed by protection → 保护 (12) and vacation → 度假 (9). Repetition is evidence of a stable translation preference in this sample, not proof that every translator made the same choice independently. Subtitle duplicates and recurring genre formulas may inflate a link.

Length and semantic field

The Length and semantic field scatter plot shows the English side averaging 58.3 characters and the Chinese side averaging 17.6 characters. Raw character counts are not directly comparable across writing systems, so the useful result is the spread, not a claim that Chinese is “one third as long”.

Institutional entries have the largest mean target length (23.1 characters), while communication entries are shortest (15.5). Legal and administrative language often needs explicit roles, conditions, or procedural detail in Chinese. Communication is more likely to remain a compact conversational action such as 解释 or 邀请.

Bilingual KWIC and suffix heat map

The Bilingual KWIC view prevents the network from becoming a list of disconnected word pairs. A short action verb only counts as an N-V shift when the aligned sentence supports that reading. For example, attention can become 注意 when a speaker asks people to notice something, but it becomes 稍息 in a military command. Both are verbal in context, but their source meaning is not identical.

The Suffix by category heat map shows why a suffix cannot serve as a category rule by itself. The same -tion family spans physical action, communication, emotion, and institutional procedure. Nominal form predicts the grammatical shift, but sentence meaning is still required to assign the semantic field.

Data-grid check

Every chart is built from the same filtered records shown in the Corpus data grid. I checked the category totals in the report against the sample JSON and used the same count function in the data build script. The grid's export button writes the current filtered records, not a hard-coded copy.

Conclusion

The project achieved a bounded, traceable corpus explorer that turns nominal-to-verbal shifts into an observable analytical process. The strongest repeated links are direct action verbs, but the sentence evidence shows why semantic context cannot be inferred from a form change alone.

The main finding is methodological as well as descriptive: a network is useful for spotting patterns, while a linked table and KWIC view are necessary for testing those patterns. The corpus supports the claim that English nominalization often reappears as a Chinese process, but it does not support treating every Chinese verb as the same kind of transposition.

Reflection

The most difficult part was not creating a chart. It was deciding what a chart should count. A large, visually attractive network can hide noisy or context-dependent links. I therefore added source-checked filters, URL state, and a visible data grid so that a reader can move from a pattern to evidence and back again.

I would improve the project by asking a second annotator to code an independent random subset and reporting agreement. I would also separate the broad “everyday” category into smaller action and state categories, but only after testing whether the extra distinction remains reliable.

AI was useful for drafting the interface, writing repeatable extraction code, and noticing duplicate or malformed text. It could not decide whether a translation is idiomatic in every context, and it initially encouraged a convenient first-500 sample. Reading the brief revealed that this would not be a fair sample. The fixed-seed resampling was a direct result of checking the brief against the proposed workflow.

學習報告

當英語名詞在中文裏重新變成行動

以翻譯系 NV Atlas 及其 OpenSubtitles 2024 語料為基礎，研究字幕中 500 組「英語名詞轉為中文動詞」的句對。

緣起 背景 研究缺口 研究問題 方法 分析 結論 反思 參考文獻

緣起

我選擇這個題目，是因為英語名詞與中文動詞之間的差異既小得可以逐句 檢查，又明顯得足以支撐一個小型研究。字幕對話亦提醒我：翻譯不是把 詞語逐字搬過去，而是因應語氣、節奏與語境，壓縮、重組及重新分配訊息。

專案由課程要求和公開的 NV Atlas 開始。我不想只把這個大型平台當成黑盒，而是抽取一個較小、可重現的 樣本，把搜尋、分類與仍然模糊的界線放在同一個網站上檢查。

背景

英語書面語常把過程壓縮成名詞，例如 the construction of the tower。Halliday 與 Matthiessen 將這種現象理解為語法隱喻：把過程重新表述為實體。中文可以保留名詞， 也可以把過程放回動詞位置，令句子更有動態感。

Vinay 與 Darbelnet 的「詞性轉換」概念，為這種局部詞類變化提供了 實用描述。本語料把範圍收窄到一個固定方向：在對齊的字幕句中，英語 名詞與中文動詞譯法形成連結。接着要問的是，哪一類意義最常被放回動詞。

研究缺口

NV Atlas 已提供大量名詞—動詞連結及多種視覺化。它的規模是優點，卻也 令訪客較難看清一個有明確界線的樣本、抽樣決定，以及分類碼本在哪些 地方出現不確定性。

本專案填補的是較小的教學缺口：公開固定隨機樣本、可重現的篩選方法、 四項透明分類，以及讓圖表觀察可以直接回到句對核對的連動介面。它不是 NV Atlas 的替代品，而是把部分來源語料整理成可攜的個案研究。

研究問題

  1. 在固定的 500 組樣本中，哪個語意場最常出現英語名詞轉為中文動詞？
  2. 重複名詞如 attention、protection、permission 的名詞—動詞連結有多一致？
  3. 原文與譯文的句長是否因語意類別而不同？
  4. 哪些碼本界線最難劃分？這些模糊處反映了甚麼？

方法

語料與抽樣

2026 年 11 月 5 日，我抽取公開 NV Atlas 搜尋頁所載的語料紀錄。 NV Atlas 列明資料來源為 OpenSubtitles 2024，並在方法說明中提及詞性 標註、Jieba 分詞、Gemma 3 輔助及人工檢查。抽取當日頁面載有 20,965 組句對紀錄。

抽樣前已固定以下篩選條件：

  • 英語與中文兩側均不可為空；
  • 英語至少有三個連續英文字母；
  • 中文一側必須含中文字元；
  • 英語句長為 10–250 字元，中文最多 300 字元；
  • 紀錄至少有一組名詞—動詞標註；
  • 刪除完全相同的英中句對。

長度條件排除 167 項極端紀錄，剩下 20,798 個候選。其後以 Python 的 random.Random(2026).sample() 不重複抽取 500 組。每條紀錄採用 NV Atlas 傳回的第一組名詞—動詞標註， 作為 key_term 及 target_key_term。資料保留句對編號、來源、 收集日期及隨機種子。

分類與核對

四類語意碼本見 分類碼本頁。分類以英語名詞在句中所側重的 意義為準，而非只看中文詞形。其後以另一固定種子 2027 選出 50 條， 對照公開來源並重新檢查分類。句對編號和文字與來源相符；檢查期間修改了 五個邊界標籤，並修正一個編碼異常。

信度限制：標註流程由 AI 協助並以規則實行。它可以 重現，但不等同獨立的人類雙重標註。口頭報告前，我仍需親自重讀 50 條 已核對紀錄，並能解釋五個邊界決定。

網站

網站由靜態 HTML、CSS、JavaScript 與 JSON 組成。搜尋及三個篩選條件 共用同一個狀態物件，任何變動都會更新網絡、散點圖、雙語 KWIC、熱圖 和資料表。點擊詞語會把它送入搜尋；網址會保存關鍵字、篩選、已選詞語、 網絡控制、排序和頁碼。

分析

樣本以日常行動與狀態居多，但這個大類本身很寬，因此總數不能直接解釋為 「日常過程更容易轉換」。類別數字必須與碼本界線及資料表一起閱讀。

名詞—動詞網絡

名詞—動詞網絡顯示重複連結。 attention → 注意 是最強的固定連線（27 次）， 其次是 protection → 保護（12 次）及 vacation → 度假（9 次）。這反映本樣本中穩定的翻譯 傾向，但不同字幕譯者不一定獨立作出相同選擇；重複字幕與類型化對白亦 可能令連結次數偏高。

長度與語意場

長度與語意場散點圖中，英語句平均 58.3 個字元，中文句平均 17.6 個字元。兩種書寫系統的字元不能直接對等， 因此重點是分佈和離散程度，而不是「中文只有英文三分一長」的結論。

制度類譯句平均最長（23.1 字元），溝通類最短（15.5 字元）。 法律與行政語言有時要在中文明示角色、條件或程序；溝通動詞則較常保持 精簡，例如 解釋 或 邀請。

雙語 KWIC 與字尾熱圖

雙語關鍵詞語境防止網絡只是一堆 脫離上下文的詞對。簡短中文動詞只有在對齊句支持下，才算名詞轉動詞的 例子。例如 attention 在提醒別人留意時譯為 注意， 但在軍事口令中可譯為 稍息。兩者都是語境中的動詞，但來源意義 不完全相同。

字尾與類別熱圖說明：單憑字尾不能 決定語意類別。同屬 -tion 的詞可以分佈在 物理行動、溝通、情感及制度程序之中。名詞形式能預測詞類轉換， 但仍須依句子意義判斷語意場。

資料表核對

所有圖表都使用語料資料表中的同一批 篩選紀錄。我以建構資料的同一套計數函數核對報告中的類別總數。資料表的 匯出按鈕寫出目前篩選結果，而不是固定副本。

結論

本專案建立了一個有明確範圍、來源可追溯的名詞—動詞語料探索器， 把英語名詞轉為中文動詞的現象變成可觀察的分析過程。最強的重複連線 多為直接行動動詞，但句子證據說明，不能只憑詞性改變推斷語意。

主要發現既有描述意義，也有方法意義：網絡有助發現規律，但必須配合 資料表與 KWIC 才能檢驗規律。語料支持「英語名詞化常以中文過程重新 出現」的說法，卻不支持把所有中文動詞都視為同一類詞性轉換。

反思

最困難的部分不是畫圖，而是決定圖表究竟要計算甚麼。華麗而龐大的網絡 可以掩蓋含糊或依賴語境的連線。因此我加入來源核對篩選、網址狀態及可見 的資料表，讓讀者能在規律與證據之間往返。

下一步應請第二位標註者獨立分類一個隨機子集，並報告一致率。我也想把 「日常」大類分成較小的行動與狀態類別，但必須先測試新增區分是否仍然可靠。

AI 有助草擬介面、編寫可重複的抽取程式，以及發現重複或受損文字；但它 不能替我判斷每個譯法在語境中是否自然。它最初也採用了方便但不公平的 「首 500 條」資料。閱讀作業要求後，我才發現這不符合抽樣原則；改用 固定種子重新抽樣，正是把作業要求與工作流程逐項核對的結果。
